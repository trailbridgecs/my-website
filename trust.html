Trust & Security

How we handle data, build responsibly, and stay accountable to the clients and Salesforce customers we work with.

How we handle data

Our engagements are built inside our clients' own Salesforce environments. We follow least-privilege access practices, work within the permissions granted for each engagement, and do not retain client data outside the systems we're contracted to work in. Where a project involves configuring integrations or AI components such as Agentforce, we document data flows clearly as part of that work, so it's always visible what moves where.

How we build

We follow secure development practices across our work: scoped permissions rather than broad access, no hardcoded credentials in code or configuration, and version-controlled, reviewed changes rather than ad hoc edits directly in production. For packaged solutions intended for AppExchange distribution, we build toward Salesforce's own Security Review standards as part of our development process.

Report a security concern

If you believe you've found a security issue related to our work or our website, we'd like to hear about it directly. Please email info@trailbridgecs.co.in with details, and we'll respond as quickly as we can.

Have questions about how we work?

We're happy to walk through our approach to security and data handling in more detail for any engagement.
